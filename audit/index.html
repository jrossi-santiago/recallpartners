    Why Two Days And Not Two Seconds

    Because a person actually writes it

    A form could hand you something instantly. It just wouldn’t be worth reading. Here’s who writes yours, and why there are only five a week.

    01

    A real person writes it.

    What you treat, how long your people have been gone, how it went the last time you contacted them — all of that changes what we’d say to them and when. Those are judgement calls, and software can’t make them. So somebody here makes them, for every practice that asks.

    02

    Five a week, and that’s the truth.

    One person writes each one, and a good one takes an hour or two. Five a week is what that comes to. If we’re full when yours comes in, the email will say so and tell you which week you’re in. No countdown clock, no fake “2 spots left”.

    03

    It’s free. Here’s why.

    We write these because some of the people who read one end up hiring us. That’s the whole catch. If there isn’t enough there to be worth doing, the page says so and we won’t chase you. Either way the page is yours — run it yourself if you like.
