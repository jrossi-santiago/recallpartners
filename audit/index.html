    Before You Type Your Email In

    Three things people ask us first

    • What does this actually cost?

      The page is free and there is nothing to buy on it. We write these because some of the people who read one end up hiring us — that’s the whole catch. If you did go on to run the campaign, you’d pay a flat fee for the five days and nothing after it: no retainer, nothing to cancel. And if there isn’t enough in your list to be worth doing, the page will say so and we won’t chase you.

    • How much work is this for me and my front desk?

      About two minutes, spread over four short screens. Nothing to install, nothing to export, no call to sit through to get the page. And if you go on to run the five days with us, there is still nothing to export: either we work inside the software you already book in, sending the messages and answering every reply ourselves so nothing lands on your front desk, or we hand you the campaign built — every message, in order — and walk you through running it on a call. Then the sending and the replies are yours, and everything before them is already done.

    • How do I know it isn’t a template with my name dropped in?

      Because a person writes it and there are only five a week. One of us goes through your answers by hand, and a good page takes an hour or two — five a week is simply what that comes to, not a countdown clock. That’s also why it takes two business days instead of two seconds: what you treat, how long your people have been gone and how the last message went are judgement calls, and software can’t make them. If we’re full when yours arrives, the email tells you which week you’re in.
