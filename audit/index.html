    Why Two Days And Not Two Seconds

    Because a person actually writes it

    There’s no version of this that a form could spit back at you instantly and still be worth reading. Here’s exactly what you’re getting, and what it costs us.

    01

    A person reads it, not a generator.

    Your treatments, your software, how long your people have been gone and what happened the last time you messaged them — all of that changes what we’d write and when we’d send it. That’s a judgement call, and a form can’t make it. So one gets made by hand for every practice that asks.

    02

    Five a week. That’s the honest number.

    One person builds each one, and a good one takes an hour or two. Five a week is what that comes to. If we’re full when yours arrives, your email will say so and tell you which week you’re in — we’re not going to sit you behind a countdown clock.

    03

    It’s free, and here’s the catch.

    There isn’t a hidden one. We build these because the conversations that come out of them are how we get work. If the numbers aren’t there, the page says so and we won’t chase you for a call. Either way the one page is yours to keep and run yourself.
