1. Who this policy covers

Recall Partners (“Recall Partners,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a client reactivation company that runs short SMS and email campaigns for med spas and aesthetic practices. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

It applies to recallpartnershq.com and to the reactivation campaigns we run on behalf of our business clients. It does not apply to the separate privacy practices of the med spas we work with, each of which maintains its own policy governing its relationship with its clients.

2. Our two roles

We handle personal information in two distinct capacities, and the rules differ:

  • As a business (a “controller”). When you visit our website, email us, call us, or book a call with us, we decide how your information is used. This policy governs that information directly.
  • As a service provider (a “processor”). When a med spa engages us to run a campaign, that practice gives us access to contact records it already holds — its own leads and past clients. The practice remains the owner of that data. We use it only to carry out the campaign we were hired to run, only on that practice’s written instructions, and for no other purpose. We do not sell it, we do not add it to any list of our own, and we do not reuse it for another client.

If you received a text or email from a med spa and want your information removed, you can reply STOP to any message, or contact us using the details in section 13 and we will pass the request to the practice and action it on our side immediately.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us directly

  • Your name, business name, email address and phone number when you book a call, email us, or call us.
  • Details you share about your practice on a call — contact list size, systems you use, treatments you offer, and what you want booked.
  • Any other information you choose to send us in correspondence.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider, which may include IP address, browser type, referring page, and the date and time of a request.
  • Google Analytics 4, which uses cookies and similar identifiers to tell us how many people visit the site, which pages they read, and roughly where they came from. It records IP address, browser and device type, referring page, and pages viewed. We use it only to understand site traffic in aggregate. Google processes this data as described in Google’s Privacy Policy, and you can opt out across all sites with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.
  • We do not run advertising trackers or retargeting pixels on this website, and we do not sell or share this data with advertisers.

Information we receive from our clients

To run a campaign, a med spa provides us with contact records for people who already have a relationship with that practice — typically first name, phone number, email address, treatment history or stated interest, and the date of last contact or visit. Our clients are responsible for the lawfulness of the data they give us and for having the relationship and consent needed to be contacted. We contact only the people on the list the client supplies. We never purchase, rent, scrape or otherwise acquire contact lists.

4. SMS and text messaging

Text messages we send on behalf of a med spa go only to people who already have an existing relationship with that practice. Every campaign follows these rules:

  • Opt out any time. Reply STOP to any message to be removed. Removal is immediate and permanent, and we scrub the practice’s existing unsubscribe list before a campaign begins.
  • Help. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at joe@recallpartnershq.com or (570) 776-5660.
  • Message frequency varies by campaign and by how you respond. A reactivation campaign runs over roughly five days.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Quiet hours are respected. Messages are sent during reasonable local hours only.

We do not share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information collected in connection with text messaging — including phone numbers and consent to receive messages — is sold, rented, or shared with any third party or affiliate for their own marketing or promotional use. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party for such purposes. Information may be shared only with the subcontracted messaging providers described in section 6, strictly to deliver the messages you have agreed to receive.

5. How we use information

  • To respond to your enquiry, hold a call with you, and prepare a proposal.
  • To plan, send, and manage a reactivation campaign for the med spa that engaged us, including replying to responses and booking appointments into that practice’s calendar.
  • To produce the wrap report we deliver to our client at the end of a campaign.
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and our own business records.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to establish or defend legal claims.

We do not use campaign contact data to market our own services, and we do not build a marketing database of our clients’ customers.

6. How we share information

We share personal information only in these circumstances:

  • With the med spa that engaged us, which owns the contact data and receives campaign results, replies, and bookings.
  • With service providers who work on our behalf under contract — messaging and email delivery platforms, our client’s own booking or marketing system, scheduling software, and website hosting. They may use the information only to perform services for us.
  • Where the law requires it, such as in response to a valid legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Recall Partners, our clients, or the public.
  • In a business transfer, if Recall Partners is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy continuing to apply.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. The mobile information restriction in section 4 applies in addition to everything in this section.

7. How long we keep it

  • Campaign contact data is deleted from our systems, or returned to the client and then deleted, within 30 days of a campaign ending, except where our client instructs otherwise in writing.
  • Opt-out records are kept indefinitely. We have to retain them so that a person who has opted out is never contacted again.
  • Enquiry and client records are kept for as long as needed to run our business and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

8. Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections, and dedicated logins scoped to a single engagement, and we revoke our access to a client’s systems at handoff. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and any relevant regulator as required by law.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you and request a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate.
  • Delete your information, subject to the retention exceptions in section 7.
  • Opt out of marketing messages at any time — reply STOP to any text, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or contact us directly.
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of, but you may still make the request.
  • Be free from discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise a right, email joe@recallpartnershq.com or call (570) 776-5660. We will verify your request and respond within the time the law allows. If your information reached us through a med spa we work with, we will also forward your request to that practice, since it is the owner of that record.

10. Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and opens our booking calendar through Cal.com. Those providers may receive your IP address and standard request data in order to serve that content. Campaign delivery uses messaging and email platforms, and in most engagements the client’s own marketing or booking system. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy, and we contract with them to restrict use of the data to the services they provide to us.

11. Children’s privacy

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The revision date at the top of this page always reflects the current version. Material changes will be posted here before they take effect.
