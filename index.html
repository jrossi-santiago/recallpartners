The Mechanism

You already paid for these people. Here’s why they never came back.

You spent money getting them in the door the first time — the ad, the lead form, the consult, the first treatment. Then life happened. They meant to rebook. Nobody followed up. And a lead that cost you $80 to generate turned into a row in a CRM nobody has opened since.

Meanwhile your front desk is running a full schedule and answering the phone. Chasing 900 dormant contacts is nobody’s job, so it never happens. The generic “We miss you! 20% off!” blast goes out once a quarter and gets ignored, because it reads like a blast.

The demand is already there. It just needs someone to go get it.

So that’s the only thing we do.
01

We only message people who already know you.

No purchased lists, no cold traffic, no scraped data. Every single person we contact opted in with you, walked through your door, or handed you their number at some point. That’s why the reply rate looks nothing like cold outreach.

02

SMS first, email right behind it.

Texts get read in minutes; email gets read when they have time. We run both on a deliberate 5-day rhythm so the same person gets a reason to reply on the channel they actually use — without ever feeling spammed.

03

Five days, then we’re gone.

This is a sprint, not a subscription. We come in, run the campaign, hand you the bookings and the reporting, and remove ourselves from your systems. You are never locked into a monthly fee to keep the results you already paid for.

You’ll know whether your database still has money in it inside five days. Not month four.

The Sprint

Your five days, hour by hour

From the call to the handoff, this is every step. There is nothing else hiding behind it.

  1. Before Day 1

    The call, and your decision

    A short call where we look at how many contacts you have, how long they’ve been dormant, and what you actually want booked. If the numbers aren’t there, we tell you and you keep your money. If they are, you get pricing and terms in writing, and you decide.

  2. Day 0 — Setup

    Access and approval

    You give us a seat in the system you already use — GoHighLevel, Boulevard, Mangomint, Podium, Zenoti, Klaviyo, whatever you run. Would you rather we stayed out of it? We build the campaign and hand you the whole thing to send yourself, and walk you through it on a call. Either way we pull the list, segment it by treatment and time since last visit, scrub opt-outs, and write the copy. You approve it once.

  3. Days 1–5 — Live

    The campaign runs

    SMS and email go out on a planned daily rhythm across the whole segmented list. Replies get answered as they come in, questions get handled, and interested people get booked directly into your calendar. You get a short report at the end of every day: sent, replied, booked.

  4. After Day 5 — Handoff

    We hand it over and get out

    Final wrap report, every booked appointment sitting in your system, the copy that worked, the objections we heard, and the confirmation script for your front desk. Then our access is revoked and we’re done. If you want another campaign in six months, you know where to find us.

Questions

Common questions

What does this cost?

You get the full number on the call, upfront and in writing, before you commit to anything. Part of what we charge is tied to appointments actually booked, so we only do well if your calendar does. There’s no retainer and no recurring charge of any kind.

Will this annoy my past clients?

It’s the first question every owner asks, and it’s the right one. These are your clients and your reputation, so the copy is written to sound like your spa reaching out, not a marketing company renting your list. Messages are specific to what each person came in for, they carry a clear opt-out, they go out during reasonable hours, and anyone who opts out is removed immediately and permanently. You approve every message before a single one sends.

What access do you actually need?

A user seat in whatever marketing or booking system you already run — enough to pull the contact list, send the campaign, and see replies. We use a dedicated login, we don’t touch anything outside the campaign, and the access is revoked at handoff on day 5.

Which systems do you work with?

GoHighLevel, Boulevard, Mangomint, Zenoti, Podium, Klaviyo, Vagaro, Aesthetic Record and most others. If you run something unusual, bring it to the call — we’ll tell you honestly on the spot whether we can work in it.

What if I don’t have a marketing system?

You probably have more of one than you think — the software you already book clients in usually sends texts and emails, and a seat in that is all we need. If it genuinely can’t send, we build the campaign and hand you the whole thing ready to go — every message, in order, with the list segmented — and walk you through running it on a call. Either way you aren’t exporting your contacts to us, and it costs you nothing extra.

Is this compliant?

We only message contacts who already have an existing relationship with your business, every message includes opt-out language, we respect quiet hours, and we scrub your existing unsubscribes before sending. That’s how we run every campaign — but we’re a marketing company, not your attorney, and your own counsel has the final word on what your business can send.

How big does my list need to be?

There’s a floor, and we’ll tell you on the call whether you clear it. Reactivation is a percentages game: too few reachable contacts and the math doesn’t work for either of us. We’ve told people to wait rather than take their money.

What happens after the five days?

You get the wrap report, the appointments, the copy that worked, and the objections we heard. Our access comes off your systems. Nothing renews and nothing bills again. Most owners let the list rest and run another campaign months later, once there’s a new group of people who’ve gone quiet.

Can I just have my front desk do this?

You can, and if they ever get to it, you should. In practice the front desk is running a full schedule, answering the phone, and checking people out. A thousand dormant contacts is a project, not a spare-afternoon task — which is exactly why that list has been sitting there for a year.

How soon can we start?

Usually within a week of the call: a day for access and segmentation, a day for copy and your approval, then the five days go live. We only run a limited number of campaigns at a time, so the calendar is the real constraint.
