I wrote it for your cancelled members specifically, so a note on the one
decision in it: it leads with a free 30-minute re-check with you rather than a
discount. People rarely quit a private gym over price. They quit because a
shoulder went, or the schedule broke, and then a few months pass and walking
back in feels like it needs a big run-up. A discount doesn't answer any of
that — it just trains the list to wait for the next one. The re-check makes the
first step small and puts them back in front of you.

Three emails over five days, the same three as texts if you message members,
and what to say when they reply — that last part is where most of these fall
over.

Two things would let me tighten it for you: roughly how many cancelled members
are on the list, and how far back you'd want to go? Someone who stopped in
March and someone who stopped in 2023 need a different opening line, and the
template handles both but you'd want to split them.
