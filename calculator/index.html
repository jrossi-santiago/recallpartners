What this calculator is actually doing

It’s arithmetic, not a model. Here’s every assumption in it, so you can argue with the ones you disagree with.

01

Dormant revenue is a multiplication.

Your dormant contact count times your average ticket. That’s it. It’s the value of one visit from every person who’s gone quiet — not lifetime value, not a projection. Nobody reactivates 100% of a list, which is exactly why we show you the conservative band too.

02

The recovery range assumes 5–10%.

Applied to your dormant contacts, not your whole database. That band is deliberately conservative for warm outreach — these are people who already gave you their number. It is a planning range, not a promise, and a list full of dead numbers will come in under it.

03

What it can’t see.

Phone validity, opt-out history, how recently you last messaged them, and whether your dormant contacts skew one-visit or ten-visit. Those swing the real number in both directions. That’s the whole reason for the call — we look at the actual list before anyone commits to anything.
