Where these numbers come from

It’s simple arithmetic, not a secret formula. Here’s every assumption behind it, so you can argue with the ones you don’t buy.

01

The big number is just multiplication.

Your quiet clients times what one visit is worth. That’s all it is. It’s what you’d take in if every one of them walked back through the door once — not over a year, once. Nobody gets all of them back, which is why we show you the realistic version underneath it.

02

The realistic range assumes 5 to 10 in every 100.

That’s a low bar on purpose. These are people who already gave you their number and already know your name, so they answer at a rate cold outreach never does. It’s still an estimate, not a guarantee — a list full of dead phone numbers will land under it.

03

What it can’t know about your list.

Whether those phone numbers still work. How long people have really been gone. Whether your quiet clients were one-visit people or ten-visit people. All of that moves the real number up or down. That’s what the call is for — we go through your actual list before anyone commits to anything.
